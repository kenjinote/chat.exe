■ Concept

The original landscape of the internet is here.
A new sensation chat app combining classic Windows 95/98 UI with modern real-time communication!

No "Likes," no "Follows," no ads, no recommendation algorithms, and no AI training data collection. We have faithfully recreated the simple, unpretentious "internets" of those days, where you never had to compare yourself to anyone.

"chat.exe" is a communication app featuring a classic design reminiscent of the early PC communication era. While adopting nostalgic gray windows and chunky buttons, it comes fully equipped with modern features like video calls, screen sharing, and voice chat.

Although the appearance is distinctly retro, the underlying video and audio compression technology has been completely rebuilt using the latest modern standards (such as H.264 hardware encoding and the Opus audio codec), ensuring highly efficient, crystal-clear, and smooth communication.

■ Features

✨ Nostalgic Retro UI
A pixel-like classic UI respecting the OS of those days. The simple gray windows elevate the nostalgic mood.

🎥 Video, Voice & Screen Share
Standard support for not just text, but high-quality video calls using webcams, voice chat, and desktop sharing.

 🎨 Free Text Formatting
Freely change bold, italic, colors, and sizes. Express yourself with the lively atmosphere of vintage chat rooms.

🚀 Ultra-Lightweight Native App
Say goodbye to heavy communication apps. Built natively in C++, it operates extremely fast while minimizing PC load.
